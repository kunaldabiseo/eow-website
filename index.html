Arizona

Daubert Affidavit / certificate
Before or at filing

The claimant certifies whether expert opinion is needed and serves a preliminary expert opinion affidavit with the initial disclosures.

A.R.S. §12-2603
Who qualifies as an expert

Licensed, with the same specialty or board certification as the defendant, and the majority of the prior year spent in clinical practice or teaching.

A.R.S. §12-2604
Evidence standard

Daubert (Ariz. R. Evid. 702, 2012)

Quick quiz · Evidence standards

Which test will the judge use on the expert?

Before a jury hears an expert, the judge decides if the testimony is allowed. States use different tests. A state name appears on the card: tap the test you think its courts use, and the answer appears with the rule behind it.

  • DaubertThe judge checks whether the expert's method is reliable. Used in federal court and most states.
  • FryeThe method must be generally accepted in the expert's field.
  • Its own testThe state uses its own rule or a mix of both.

Expert ophthalmology witness FAQ

Answers before the first call

Q.01What does an ophthalmology expert witness do?

An ophthalmology expert witness reviews the medical records in an eye care case, gives an opinion on whether the care met the accepted standard and whether any breach caused the injury, and explains that opinion in reports, affidavits, depositions and at trial.

Was this helpful?
Q.02What services does your retainer cover as an ophthalmology expert witness?

The retainer covers review of the medical records, and drafting of a report with my opinions regarding the case.

Was this helpful?
Q.03What is the best way to send medical records for your review?

You can send medical records by paper, email, flash drive, CD or Dropbox. Please send case details first, so the conflict check is done before any records arrive.

Was this helpful?
Q.04I have a case running close to the statute of limitation regarding potential ophthalmology malpractice, how quickly can you prepare a report?

Only the attorney can advise on the deadline itself. Put the filing date in your first message so timing for the review and report can be confirmed straight away.

Experience, and reading available medical literature to help determine the forensic aspects of the case.

Yes. I can perform an IME in Phoenix, Arizona.

Q.10How does an expert ophthalmology witness determine the cause of an eye injury?

An expert ophthalmologist witness determines the cause of an eye injury by examining medical records, including eye exams and imaging results, and taking into account other relevant factors such as previous medical history and the circumstances surrounding the injury.

Was this helpful?
Q.11What are the qualifications and experience needed to become an expert ophthalmology witness?

To become an ophthalmology expert witness, one must possess board certification in ophthalmology and have substantial clinical and surgical experience as well as expertise in the field. It is also crucial for the expert to have an understanding of the legal processes and procedures involved in expert witness testimony. The expert ophthalmology witness should have good judgment, be fair, and ethical.

Was this helpful?
Q.12How does an expert ophthalmology witness help in a legal case?

An expert ophthalmology witness helps in a legal case by providing an unbiased evaluation of the eye injury and its cause. They offer testimony in court or depositions to support or refute the claims being made and provide clarification of complex medical concepts for the judge and jury.

Was this helpful?
Q.13How does an expert ophthalmology witness work with lawyers and the court system?

An expert ophthalmology witness works with lawyers and the court system by reviewing case materials and medical records provided by the lawyers. They communicate with the lawyers to provide answers to questions and clarify any issues. They also can provide testimony in court or participate in depositions as required.

Was this helpful?
Q.14What is the process for retaining an expert ophthalmology witness for a legal case?

The process of hiring an expert ophthalmology witness for a legal case begins by recognizing the requirement for an expert witness. Then, it is essential to find an expert in ophthalmology with the appropriate qualifications and experience. Once the appropriate expert is located, the lawyer will get in touch with them to discuss the case and secure their services. The expert's fee is typically required upfront, and the lawyer will provide the records to the expert for review, either through certified mail or digitally.

Was this helpful?
Q.15What records are needed to start a review?

The complete office chart, operative and anesthesia reports, signed consent forms, imaging and test data such as OCT, visual fields, topography and biometry, medication records, follow-up notes and records from any other treating providers.

Was this helpful?
Q.16Does Dr. Lefkowitz review cases in my state?

He reviews cases for attorneys across the United States. Each state has its own pre-suit and expert qualification rules, which you can look up on this page.

Was this helpful?
Q.17Can he provide the affidavit, certificate or expert report my state requires?

He prepares written opinions and reports for the cases he takes on. Include your state and any filing deadline in your first message so the format and timing can be confirmed before the review starts.

Was this helpful?
Q.18How are fees handled?

Fee terms are shared directly when you send your case details, before any records are reviewed.

Was this helpful?
Q.19Can an ophthalmologist testify about HGN field sobriety tests?

Yes. An ophthalmologist can explain how nystagmus works, how the horizontal gaze nystagmus test should be given and which medical conditions can cause nystagmus unrelated to alcohol.

Was this helpful?
